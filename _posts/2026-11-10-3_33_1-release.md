FreeRDP version 3.33.1 is released:

As usual, the archive can be downloaded from:

https://pub.freerdp.com/releases/

Bugfix and papercut release

* Improved shadow server:
  * faster encoding by avoiding unchanged areas
  * back pressure from client: if it can not keep up with in flight frames
    reduce quality and do not send new frames
  * windows backend improvements
  * mac os backend improvements
* hashless certificate support (Ed25519, Ed448, ML-DSA, SLH-DSA)
* apple videotoolbox h264 encoder/decoder support
* various RAILS fixes (x11 and sdl)
